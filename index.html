About Me

Hi, I'm Chaitanya Sharma a Product Manager, builder, storyteller, and AI enthusiast based in New Delhi, India. I've worked on everything from NLP spell checkers and zero-code website platforms to data-driven consulting projects and sustainable fashion models. My work has spanned product management, marketing, AI, and strategic research for names like IMDb, Dell, and Lenskart. Along the way, I've picked up crucial conversational skills and a strong knack for making sense of chaos.
