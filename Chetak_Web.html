About Me

Hi, I'm Chaitanya Sharma a Product Manager, builder, storyteller, and AI enthusiast based in New Delhi, India. I've worked on everything from NLP spell checkers and zero-code website platforms to data-driven consulting projects and sustainable fashion models. My work has spanned product management, marketing, AI, and strategic research for names like IMDb, Dell, and Lenskart. Along the way, I've picked up skills in Python, SQL, Jira, Figma, and a strong knack for making sense of chaos. When I'm not analyzing user flows or pitching roadmaps, you'll find me jamming to Green Day, debating tech's future, cycling through NCR, or dreaming up my next big idea. This site is my digital home ; where I showcase my work, share thoughts. Let's build something awesome.
